Our story

A small room,
a slow afternoon.

静けさ

We named our shop Fuji (不二)—a Japanese expression meaning "not two," or "peerless." Nothing else quite like it. That idea is the vision behind everything we do.

We didn't want to open just another coffee shop; we wanted to build something distinct that stands apart from the rest, one quiet, unhurried place in the middle of the day that asks nothing of you but to sit for a while.

Everything here is crafted in small batches with that same standard in mind. Milkshakes are blended to order, coffee is pulled shot by shot, and cheesecakes are baked the night before so they set slowly. There is no rush, and there are no shortcuts. Just a window, something cold, and a space built to be one of a kind.

“The best order is the one you take your time with.”
